About Us

A home kitchen, not a factory

Chubby Baker & Co started as a home-kitchen baking project in Bandra West and grew into one of the neighbourhood's go-to spots for custom cakes and small-batch desserts — built entirely on word of mouth, no walk-in counter, no shortcuts.

Fresh From the Oven

Every Tier, Done Right

Made for the Moment

Every order is made after you reach out — on WhatsApp, Instagram or by phone — so nothing sits around waiting to be sold. The menu leans into proper, buttery bakes: burnt basque cheesecake, lasso shortbreads, rich chocolate cakes, and lighter options like millet cakes for anyone watching sugar.

"Not overly sweet, beautifully packaged, and made like someone's actually paying attention — that's the whole idea."

We're a small team, so every cake gets looked at properly before it goes out the door — right down to the box it's packed in.
