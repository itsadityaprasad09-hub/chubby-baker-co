About Us

A home kitchen, not a factory

Chubby Baker & Co is a home-style bakery in Bandra West, Mumbai, specialising in custom cakes and small-batch bakes — from burnt basque cheesecake to buttery lasso shortbreads.

There's no walk-in counter — every order is made after you reach out on WhatsApp or by phone, so it's baked fresh, close to when you need it.

"Slow bakes, real butter, and plenty of care — that's the whole recipe."
