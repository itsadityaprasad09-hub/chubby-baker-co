our story

Baked with care, one order at a time.

Chubby Baker & Co started as a home kitchen experiment in Bandra West — a way to bring proper, unhurried baking to a neighbourhood full of quick bites. What began with a handful of custom cake orders for friends has grown into a small, dedicated bakery known for its burnt basque cheesecake and buttery lasso shortbreads.

We don't keep a walk-in counter. Every order — whether it's a birthday cake or a box of shortbread — is made after you reach out, so it's baked fresh, close to when you need it.

"No pre-mixes, no shortcuts. If it takes an extra hour to get the texture right, we give it that hour."

"Every cake is a small celebration — we just make sure it tastes as good as it looks."— Chubby Baker & Co
