our story

Small kitchen, made-to-order.

Baked, not mass-produced.

Chubby Baker & Co. runs out of a home kitchen in Mount Mary, Bandra West. We don't keep a display case — every cake, cheesecake and box of shortbread is started only once an order comes in, which is why we ask for a short call or WhatsApp message before baking begins.

Custom cakes are our specialty. That usually means a quick back-and-forth on the theme, size and flavour, a rough sketch if the design is elaborate, and then hands-on baking and piping in-house — no outsourced decoration.

How an order works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with the occasion, date and any design ideas.
  2. We confirm size, flavour and price, and lock in a slot — custom cakes need advance notice.
  3. Your cake is baked, decorated and boxed by hand for pickup or delivery.
